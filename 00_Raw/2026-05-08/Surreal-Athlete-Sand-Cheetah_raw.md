# [[Surreal Athlete & Sand Cheetah]]

## 📌 Brief Summary
A high-octane cinematic concept art brief depicting a male athlete sprinting across desert dunes, pursued by a kaiju-scale cheetah entity composed entirely of swirling sand and dust — designed for epic sports brand advertising campaigns.

## 📖 Core Content

### Subject
A lean, muscular male athlete in his late 20s, tanned and sweat-glistened, captured mid-stride in a forward-leaning sprint across a sand dune. Expression locked in intense determination, mouth slightly open in exertion. Dressed in a light grey athletic t-shirt (customizable brand logo on chest) and matching shorts.

### The Sand Cheetah Entity
Not a physical animal — a **volumetric simulation** of millions of sand grains, dust particles, and wind. A kaiju-sized cheetah head and front paw emerge from the sandstorm, dwarfing the runner. Fur edges dissolve seamlessly into the surrounding storm. The athlete's kicked-up sand trail visually connects him to the creature, implying he is both running from it and born of it.

### Environment & Composition
| Element | Detail |

| Setting | Vast Sahara-style desert under partly cloudy blue sky |
| Camera | 24mm wide-angle cinema prime, low angle, f/8, tracking shot |
| Depth layers | Runner (FG) → Sand Cheetah (MG) → Sky (BG) |
| Movement | Left-to-right dynamic sprint |
| Lens effect | Motion blur on edges, deep depth of field |

### Lighting
- **Key light:** Harsh sun, top-left, warm bright white daylight
- Illuminates the runner's profile and the textured ridges of the cheetah's face
- Deep sharp shadows define sand ripples and muscle definition
- Atmospheric haze from the sandstorm; sun flares peek through clouds

### Color Grade
Desaturated blues, rich earthy beiges and tans, high contrast — evoking both the heat of the desert and the cinematic intensity of a sports campaign.
