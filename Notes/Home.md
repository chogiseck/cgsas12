# Notes

Welcome to your Obsidian vault. This is your personal knowledge management system.

## Quick Navigation

- [[Inbox/]] — Capture new ideas and notes here first
- [[Projects/]] — Active projects you're working on
- [[Areas/]] — Ongoing responsibilities and areas of life
- [[Resources/]] — Reference material by topic
- [[Archive/]] — Completed or inactive items

## Getting Started

1. **Capture** — Drop new notes into [[Inbox/]] without worrying about organization
2. **Process** — Regularly move notes from Inbox into the appropriate folder
3. **Organize** — Link related notes using `[[wiki links]]`
4. **Review** — Periodically review Projects and Areas

## Recent Notes
